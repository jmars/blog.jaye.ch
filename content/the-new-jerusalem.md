# The New Jerusalem

### After an ideology fell, a country without a frame — and the group that supplied one, and claimed the nation as the world's sacred centre

*Seventh of the case studies. [The method post](/the-environment/) set the rule; [the empty
vessel](/the-empty-vessel/) took the mechanism outside Brazil into a culture that already had the
safeguard. This one takes it to the opposite condition — a country that had just lost its frame
entirely. Three environments, one mechanism.*

*Status.* The events are documented and adjudicated; the reading of them through this series' model
is an **inference**, offered as one. The post relies on two recent academic studies of the movement
(a 2026 Kyiv study of its doctrine and ritual; a 2026 Calgary thesis on its rituals) and on the trial
record. It names the founders because a court convicted them. As everywhere in this series: a
description of an environment is not a finding about anyone's mind.

---

## 1. What the environment supplies when it has nothing to supply

The Brazilian cases argued that an environment can **pre-supply** the levers a predator otherwise has
to build. Japan supplied a safeguard a group had to **overthrow**. Both are environments with content.

This case is the third condition, and it is the one that makes the argument general:

> An environment can also supply **nothing** — and a vacuum of meaning is itself a lever.

In 1991 the Soviet Union ended. Across its former republics, the frame that had organised public life
for seventy years stopped, and nothing replaced it. A Ukrainian academic account of the movement that
appeared in that gap describes the condition plainly: the group emerged as "an expression of spiritual
crisis and the transformation of religiosity in post-Soviet Ukraine," a "search for new forms of
sacrality **after the collapse of totalitarian ideology**," while "exposing the dangers of religious
manipulation during times of social transformation."[^valiukh]

Read that as a specification of the environment, and every clause is a lever:

| what the collapse left | what it does for a group |
|---|---|
| no organising frame | the group's frame faces **no competitor** |
| a population trained to believe collectively | belief itself is intact — only its object is missing |
| a nation in an identity crisis | a cosmic role for the nation is **available to be granted** |
| no reliable authorities | the authority slot is **empty and unguarded** |

Brazil's environment did the predator's framing **for** it. Ukraine's did something subtler: it left
the **demand** — the hunger for a frame — and let one group supply the whole thing. It is the coercion
post's selection criterion — *no holder present* — raised from a person to a country.

---

## 2. The raw material that was still there

A vacuum is not an absence of culture, and this is where the case gets specific. What remained in
Ukraine was not nothing; it was **material**, and some of it was unusually well suited to what came
next:

- **An eschatological inheritance.** A society formed by Orthodox Christianity does not need
  eschatology explained. The vocabulary of apocalypse, resurrection, the Mother of God, and a chosen
  people was culturally **resident** — and could be re-pointed without translation.
- **A national sacred geography.** The movement's central claim was not merely doctrinal. Per the
  Kyiv study, its teaching "proclaimed Ukraine as the sacred centre of world renewal and **Kyiv as the
  'New Jerusalem'**," with a female messiah — the "**Mother of the World**" — embodying the dawn of an
  "**Age of Light**."[^valiukh] That is the messianic slot of [the method post](/the-environment/) —
  the awaited figure who arrives with authority no one granted him — given a **national address**. The group did not
  ask for a following; it offered a **country** a role in the cosmos.
- **A traumatised population.** The decade's injuries — the collapse, the economic shock, and in
  Ukraine the still-live memory of Chernobyl — supplied a great deal of suffering looking for a
  meaning.

So the environment's contribution was not a lever set. It was a **market**: a hungry population, a
resident vocabulary, and an empty chair. And [the case studies](/the-environment/) have already shown
what an empty chair is worth.

---

## 3. The group that filled it

The movement called itself the **Great White Brotherhood YUSMALOS** — the acronym compounding its three
divine components: *YUS* for its founder, *MA* for its prophet, *LOS* for the planetary Logos.

**Yuri Krivonogov** — *Yuoan Swami* — was a cybernetics engineer and, in the phrase the record uses, a
**"specialist in psychological influence."** He had been in the International Society for Krishna
Consciousness and was expelled for claiming to be Krishna's avatar; in 1988 he founded an "Institute
of Man" and lectured on **meditation and spiritual practices**.[^wiki]

**Marina Tsvigun** — *Maria Devi Christos* — was a former Komsomol journalist, who claimed a
near-death experience and was proclaimed by the movement as the **"Living God"**, the female messiah
and Mother of the World.[^wiki] The group promised a Golden Age to exactly **144,000** elect — the
"Yusmalians"— and taught that the others would be destroyed by *fohata*, a divine energy and its
"fohatic radiation."[^fohata]

Two things about that pair are worth pausing on. The one who could **read and shape states** supplied
the **technique**; the one who was **proclaimed divine** supplied the **endpoint**. That division of
labour is the coercion post's structure exactly — and note that neither was the source of the demand.
The demand was already there.

## 4. The practice: what is documented, and what is inferred

This is where the case meets [the mechanism post](/meditation-harm/) — and where it stops short of
it, and the stopping point should be said plainly rather than papered over. **The practice and the
frame are documented. The interior state is not.** There is no account here of the dissociative
phenomenology the Aum case supplies in its founder's own words; what follows is a practice, a graded
path and an appraisal, and the step from those to the collapse is an **inference** — stated as one,
and not leaned on harder than it will bear.

What *is* documented is the practice, and it is not a generic coercion inventory. The Kyiv study finds
that the movement combined Christian eschatology, esoteric cosmology, occult feminism and
**"psychotechnical practices"** — a study of the movement's rituals, themselves "underexamined in
Western scholarship," reads them as bound up with conversion[^boots] — and that its
conclusions concern a wider shift: "from religion of belief to **religion of experience**, where ritual
serves as a tool of **inner transformation**."[^valiukh]

That is the mechanism's own description of itself — the shift from *believing* to *undergoing*, with
the ritual built to produce the undergoing. On that, the coercion post's inventory follows as the
record gives it:

- **A graded path of self-transformation** — meditation taught as the route in; the state itself as the
  goal.
- **Ascetic pressure on the second axis** — **fasting** and hunger strikes appear throughout the
  record (detained members went on hunger strike; the movement's practice was marked by fasting) —
  lowering damping, moving the system toward the cliff.[^palmer]
- **Milieu control** — the renunciation of family ties, the donation of property and money to the
  Brotherhood, the wearing of white; recruitment of the young, including minors.[^wiki]
- **A structure built to hold it** — a "strict hierarchy and discipline," operating, per the
  investigator, in **cells of five** relaying information up to the founders.[^wiki]

And the appraisal — the move that makes the state un-rescuable — was supplied by the doctrine itself:
the collapse is **the Age of Light**; those outside are not merely wrong but about to be **destroyed**
by radiation only the elect will survive. Every warning sign arrives already re-coded as election.

---

## 5. The endpoint, and what the state asked for

A frame that owns the ending sets the date. The movement's apocalypse was announced for **24
November 1993** and revised repeatedly as it approached (Palmer's record of the leaflets has
"countdown" dates of 24, then 14, then 11 November);[^palmer] the gathering was fixed for **10
November 1993**, when its members attempted to take **Saint Sophia's Cathedral in Kyiv** — with, by
the record, a plan to kill Tsvigun and then
self-immolate inside it, in the belief that she would **rise on the third day** to judge the
world.[^wiki]

This is the mechanism post's trap made liturgical: **the state that most needs care is the state that
feels like the destination.** The members were not walking toward a danger; they were walking toward a
resurrection. Police intervened; some six hundred members were detained; twenty-five were charged; the
detained went on hunger strike, and stood down only on Tsvigun's word.[^wiki]

The adjudication came in 1996: Tsvigun **four years**, Krivonogov **seven**, a third leader six, for
seizing public buildings and endangering citizens "under the guise of religious rites."[^wiki]

Note what closed the case, because the series keeps returning to it: **not a better idea, and not the
frame's own self-correction — a raid and a law.** The state that had vacated the frame supplied the
check the frame could not.

## 6. The counterweights, and one collision worth avoiding

Three, and the first prevents a real error.

**A different group has almost the same name.** The **Bulgarian White Brotherhood** — the
*Bălgaro Bialo Bratstvo* of Peter Deunov, a Rosicrucian-influenced order founded in 1900 and still
practising its *paneurhythmy* in the Rila mountains — is **not** this movement. It shares the name and
nothing else. Any account that merges them is simply wrong, and the resemblance is a trap the series'
own rule about resolving names before using them is designed to catch.[^bulgarian]

**This was also a panic, and the panic did harm of its own.** The case became one of the
post-Soviet world's defining "sect" scares; a substantial scholarly literature reads it through
**stigma** and the media's role, and the movement's name became a byword for a hazard.[^palmer] That is
[the label post](/the-label/)'s territory: the group did documented harm and was also made an object,
and the two facts do not cancel. It is worth saying plainly that a case study is not a licence to
generalise from it to every unfamiliar group that follows.

**And an honest limit on the mechanism claim.** The practices here — meditation, fasting, ecstatic
ritual, the "religion of experience" — are documented, and they are the same territory as the
mechanism. But the *specific* dissociative phenomenology that the Aum case supplies in the founders'
own words (the guru's clone, the staged kundalini) is **not** documented this precisely for the White
Brotherhood. What is documented is the **practice and the frame**; the interior states of the members
are inferred, not measured. The post's claim should be held at that strength.

---

## 7. What this case adds

The series' taxonomy — Brazil *given*, Japan *guarded*, Ukraine *absent*, India-to-the-West
*built* — and the finding that the **environment sets the price rather than the choice**, are
stated in full in [the series opener](/the-same-move/). This case's own contribution is the third
square: an environment that supplies **nothing**, and the discovery that a vacuum is a lever, because
a population that has just lost its frame is the most willing buyer of a new one.
