# The Assumed Seat

### A seat nobody claimed, and a provocation smaller than the reaction it draws — two clauses whose joint makes an objection unwinnable, assembled from fields that do not cite one another

*[The operative master](/the-operative-master/) documented a fit that succeeds: an operator reads a frame he does not share, finds the position it leaves open, and occupies it, so the target's own frame does the installing. [Ahead of the story](/ahead-of-the-story/) documented the fit that is refused: the target stands outside, the move inverts, and the target's own accurate report becomes the evidence against them. This post is the third case, and it has no moment in it: the position is occupied by **assumption**. Nobody claims it, nothing is asserted, and there is nothing to point at because nothing was said. What makes that a structure rather than a mood is the second clause, and the two are dangerous only together. A seat held by assumption makes an objection **illegitimate** — you are contesting something nobody said, and must justify yourself against a default. A provocation sized **below** the reaction it draws makes an illegitimate objection **unwinnable** — the room tracks arousal, not cause, and the live question silently changes from *did he do something?* to *why is that person so angry?*. [The owned cosmos](/the-owned-cosmos/) named the seat and the endpoint that stations someone at it; this is that seat occupied without the claim. [The costume](/the-costume/) drew the line this blog depends on — that levers without the mechanism are power and not predation — and the boundary below holds that line rather than moving it.*

*Status.* The literature assembled here is quoted as its authors wrote it, and what was actually read for each quotation is in the notes. What this post adds, as an **inference** offered as one, is that the fragments below are pieces of a **single move in two clauses**, and that the technique is the **ratio** between them rather than either part. It names no living person's conduct and diagnoses no one; it describes a **structure**. It is not a diagnostic instrument and not a cult-detector: §10 says why the absence of a gauge is a property of the subject rather than a courtesy. **No intention is required** for the mechanism. The effect it describes is real; only the attribution of **cause** is false.

## 1. The third case

Each of the two previous posts had an event in it. Neither is necessary here. There is a seat — a place where someone's standing is treated as settled — and a second thing: the person in it can be made to look provoked into nothing.

> **One.** A position held by **assumption** rather than by claim makes objection illegitimate: contesting it means contesting something that was never said, which puts the burden of justification on the objector, against a default nobody has had to defend.

> **Two.** A provocation sized **below** the reaction it produces makes an illegitimate objection unwinnable: the room reads the size of the reaction, not the size of the cause, so the question on the floor changes from what was done to what is wrong with the person objecting.

Neither clause is unusual alone, and both are documented — in literatures that mostly do not cite one another. The move is not unnamed because it is hidden. It is unnamed because its name is scattered.

---

## 2. Clause one, where the field that names it first is sociology

A shared definition of a situation can be held by nobody in particular because it is almost never asserted. The projections of the participants are "sufficiently attuned to one another so that open contradiction will not occur," and what the attunement consists of is the sentence that matters:

> "Rather, each participant is expected to suppress his immediate heartfelt feelings, conveying a view of the situation which he feels the others will be able to find at least temporarily acceptable. The maintenance of this surface of agreement, this veneer of consensus, is facilitated by each participant concealing his own wants behind statements which assert values to which everyone present feels obliged to give lip service."[^goffman1959]

and what the surface amounts to:

> "Together the participants contribute to a single over-all definition of the situation which involves not so much a real agreement as to what exists but rather **a real agreement as to whose claims concerning what issues will be temporarily honored**. ... I will refer to this level of agreement as a 'working consensus.'"[^goffman1959]

That is not a shared belief about what is true but an arrangement about **whose account will count** — the seat, standing on nothing but the ordinary preference for not making a scene. Goffman describes how it is distributed:

> "Further, there is usually a kind of **division of definitional labor**. Each participant is allowed to establish the tentative official ruling regarding matters which are vital to him but not immediately important to others ... In exchange for this courtesy he remains silent or non-committal on matters important to others but not immediately important to him."[^goffman1959]

A seat is often not taken. It is *left*: everyone grants the person standing there their official ruling about their own matter, in exchange for the same silence on their own. A thing nobody contests has not been asserted; it has merely not been denied. Breaching the arrangement is where objection gets expensive, and Goffman's account of the **remedial interchange** has the repair performed by **the person who breached it**:

> "Note that the offender's willingness to initiate and perform his own castigation has certain unapparent values. ... Apologies represent a splitting of the self into a blameworthy part and a part that stands back and sympathizes with the blame giving, and, by implication, is worthy of being brought back into the fold."[^goffman1971]

The objection has cost something before anyone answers it, and the seat has not had to say a word. Goffman also notes that an argument can only be held if the participants "must be careful to agree not to disagree on the proper tone of voice, vocabulary, and degree of seriousness in which all arguments are to be phrased."[^goffman1959] The manner of the objection is part of what must be agreed — the hinge to §4.

## 3. Clause one, where it is named as a tactic and measured

A second literature names the person who exploits the arrangement, with a scale — which is why it is cited here rather than the moral vocabulary. Christie and Geis's *Studies in Machiavellianism* opens with a description rather than a judgement:

> "Since the publication of The Prince in 1532, the name of its author has come to designate the use of guile, deceit, and opportunism in interpersonal relations. Traditionally, the 'Machiavellian' is someone who views and manipulates others for his own purposes."[^mach]

Their four requirements for the manipulator read as a job description for the seat, the last being the clause-one property exactly:

> "1. A relative lack of affect in interpersonal relationships. ... 2. A lack of concern with conventional morality. ... 3. A lack of gross psychopathology. ... 4. Low ideological commitment. The essence of successful manipulation is a focus upon getting things done rather than a focus upon long-range ideological goals."[^mach]

The published instrument makes the tactic concrete — "It is wise to flatter important people"[^mach4] — and coalition-building is measured in the same volume: Geis's Con Game was built as "a conflict of interest situation in a small group in which psychological pressure tactics, effectively employed, could influence the distribution of rewards", whose winners were those most successful "in getting others to cooperate — at their own expense."[^mach] A construct with items and a score is the opposite of an accusation: the behaviour has a published description, so a move can be described without the mover being diagnosed.[^darktriad]

The organisational side names the same property from another direction, and names **deniability** outright. Eisenberg's account of strategic ambiguity identified four functions of deliberate vagueness, and his own later retrospective lists them: "its capacity to promote unified diversity, to preserve privileged positions, **to foster deniability**, and to facilitate organizational change."[^eisenberg]

Lifton supplies the same property at the scale of a vocabulary. **Loading the language**: "The language of the totalist environment is characterized by the thought-terminating cliché. The most far-reaching and complex of human problems are compressed into brief, highly reductive, definitive-sounding phrases, easily memorized and easily expressed."[^lifton] And **doctrine over person**: "the underlying assumption is that the doctrine ... is ultimately more valid, true, and real than is any aspect of actual human character or human experience."[^lifton] At the scale of one room: the vocabulary for describing what just happened has already been assigned, so one's own account of it is the *least* authoritative account available. Lifton also supplies the mechanism by which an objection becomes a personal defect — the environment "counters such 'deviant' tendencies with the accusation that they stem entirely from personal 'problems' ... derived from untoward earlier ('bourgeois') influences."[^lifton]

Fromm supplies the piece that keeps all of this from becoming a claim about a soul: "Character is the specific structure in which human energy is organized in the pursuit of man's goals; ... To use Heraclitus's phrase, character is man's fate."[^fromm] That is why §7 can say **no intention is required** and mean it structurally: conduct that runs a tactic is not evidence of having chosen it.

## 4. Clause two, where the field that names it first is communication theory

The second clause needed a description of a situation in which every response loses, and it exists. It was written in 1956 by Bateson, Jackson, Haley and Weakland about the family situation of schizophrenia, and the provenance needs stating so the borrowing is honest: the theory has been contested and nothing here claims otherwise.[^bateson] What it supplies is a **formal structure**:

> "From this theory and from observations of schizophrenic patients is derived a description, and the necessary conditions for, a situation called the 'double bind' — a situation in which no matter what a person does, he 'can't win.'"[^bateson]

Then the ingredients, where the crucial point is a matter of **levels** and not of contradiction:

> "4. A secondary injunction conflicting with the first **at a more abstract level**, and like the first enforced by punishments or signals which threaten survival. ... 5. A tertiary negative injunction prohibiting the victim from escaping from the field."[^bateson]

A pair of contradictory instructions can be argued with; an instruction and a *higher-order* instruction about how to take it cannot, because the level at which the argument would be held is the level the higher-order instruction has already occupied. And the outcome is the ratio in one line: "The child is punished for discriminating accurately what she is expressing, and he is punished for discriminating inaccurately — he is caught in a double bind."[^bateson]

The third move closes the field, and escape is a move within the system and is read there:

> "The only way the child can really escape from the situation is to comment on the contradictory position his mother has put him in. However, if he did so, the mother would take this as an accusation that she is unloving and both punish him and insist that his perception of the situation is distorted."[^bateson]

The prohibition is on **metacommunication** — talking about the exchange rather than inside it — and the consequence is the paper's most useful sentence: "he cannot choose the one alternative which would help him to discover what people mean; ... the human being is like any self-correcting system which has lost its governor."[^bateson] That is the countermeasure's foundation, and §11 returns to it. The objection this post is about is a metacommunicative statement — *this is what you are doing, and here is the size of it* — and that is the move the message forbids.

## 5. Clause two, where the same structure is named in four other literatures

**The provocation, measured as supervisory behaviour.** Tepper's **abusive supervision** is defined as "subordinates' perceptions of the extent to which supervisors engage in the sustained display of hostile verbal and nonverbal behaviors, excluding physical contact",[^tepper] and it is operationalised as a fifteen-item frequency scale whose items are the behaviour itself: "Ridicules me"; "Expresses anger at me when he/she is mad for another reason."[^tepper] The last is the ratio in miniature: the cause is elsewhere and the anger is delivered here, so the person receiving it has nothing to respond *to*. Ashforth's **petty tyranny** covers the same ground: "the tendency to lord one's power over others."[^ashforth] Its items ask whether a manager "[y]ells at subordinates" (a belittling item) or "[u]ses authority or position for personal gain" (an arbitrariness and self-aggrandizement item), and among its dimensions is *noncontingent punishment* — a penalty that follows from nothing the person did.[^ashforthitems]

**The manner, measured as the reason a claim is dismissed.** In philosophy this is **testimonial injustice**: "Testimonial injustice occurs when prejudice causes a hearer to give a deflated level of credibility to a speaker's word."[^fricker] Where the deflation is triggered by the manner — by the speaker sounding angry — the term is **tone policing**, and it has a peer-reviewed literature. Bailey treats it as silencing: "I focus on tone policing and tone vigilance to illustrate the relationship between silencing and angry knowledge management."[^bailey] Biddle and Hufnagel described a school's handling of student voice as preferring "polite expressions over overtly emotional or negative feelings expressed about school."[^biddle] The manner becomes a condition of the hearing, so the substance is never reached.

**The reaction, measured — and its deniability.** Indirect aggression is "a set of circuitous strategies that implicate peers as a means to sabotage the victim's social relationships and self-esteem" — and then the sentence that is the whole clause: "The indirect nature of the aggressive act often enables the aggressor to remain unidentified, thereby avoiding a counterattack from the victim and disapproval from other peers or adults."[^brendgen] The construct was established in peer-reviewed work on aggression in children,[^bjorkqvist] and its sibling, relational aggression, was introduced to explain a systematic undercount: that "boys are more aggressive than girls" reflected "a lack of research on forms of aggression that are relevant to young females rather than an actual gender difference in levels of overall aggressiveness."[^crick] The undercount and the deniability are one fact from two sides.

And the register this post is written in is Sweet's: "This article argues that gaslighting is primarily a sociological rather than a psychological phenomenon." Her definition is the one to carry forward: "I define gaslighting as a set of attempts to create a 'surreal' (Ferraro 2006) social environment by making the other in an intimate relationship seem or feel 'crazy.'"[^sweet] **Not a personality, a position.**

## 6. The live question, and how it changes

The room attends to the largest visible event, which is the objector's state: the hearer's assessment of the *speaker* substitutes for an assessment of the *claim*. The abuse literature has a name for the ratio, and it is **contested**. The term is **reactive abuse** — a person is deliberately provoked until they react, and the reaction is then cited as the abuse. It is not a settled clinical construct. It appears in the peer-reviewed literature as one category within perpetrator typologies, among those that split by "gender and motivation e.g. controlling, situational or reactive abuse", and the same review records that such typologies "have themselves been criticised" for presenting their groups "as if they were fully discrete."[^gilchrist] The sharper criticisms of the term come from advocacy and clinical writing rather than journals, and this post cites them as what they are.[^hotline] **The term names the ratio precisely, and the construct is unsettled.**

The settled construct beside it is Johnson's **violent resistance** — "in which one partner is predominantly fighting back to protect him or herself."[^johnson] A frequency count cannot separate a resistance from a campaign, so the categories split by motive and control rather than by count: the same problem the ratio creates in a room. The reversal has a name this blog has already used — DARVO, deny, attack, reverse victim and offender.[^darvo] Unlike DARVO, which is something a person **does**, the reversal here is performed by the room, by attention falling on a large visible reaction, and the person in the seat need not have said anything. There is nobody at the point where the reversal happens.

## 7. The joint, and the one false thing

Neither clause works alone. **Without the seat, the provocation is merely unpleasant**: anyone can provoke, and a person who provokes and gets an angry response is a person others find difficult, which rooms handle by ordinary means. **Without the provocation, the seat is vacated the first time someone disagrees aloud**: a working consensus that is never tested is not exploitable by anyone, and it becomes a structure when disagreement is made to cost more than the seat. So the technique is the **ratio** — not the small act, which is nothing, and not the large reaction, which on its own is a fact about the person having it, but the relation between the two. A relation has no author present in the room.

**The effect is not fake.** The person who has been provoked is genuinely dysregulated — genuinely angry, genuinely shaken, genuinely less able to be measured in the next minute than in the last. What is false is a single attribution: the claim that the size of the reaction is evidence about the cause. The state can be genuine and its interpretation installed, so the honest sentence is not *nothing happened to you*. It is *the size of what you are feeling is being used as the measurement of what was done, and it is not a measurement of that*.

**And no intention is required.** A person who holds a seat and is genuinely tolerant, genuinely unhurried, genuinely above the exchange is not running a technique; they are conduct inside a structure, and the structure does not ask them to intend anything. What makes it work is not the occupant's plan but the two clauses and the room's ordinary habits: a preference for not making a scene, and attention's preference for the largest visible event.

## 8. The closed exits, one at a time

That every exit closes is a strong claim, so it is paid for one at a time, with the clause that closes it named.

**The position cannot be contested.** There is no proposition to dispute, only a standing arrangement about whose account counts, maintained by everyone's silence. Disputing it requires first establishing that there is one, which is itself a metacommunicative move.

**The person cannot be contested.** Commenting on the position "would be taken as an accusation that she is unloving", and the response would be to "both punish him and insist that his perception of the situation is distorted."[^bateson] Objecting to a good man is the objector's defect: it is what happens when a claim about conduct arrives at a level where the person's goodness is the premise.

**The erudition cannot be contested.** A seat can be held partly by a diffuse warrant — a broad reading, a range of allusion, a fluency hard to check and impossible to be sure about. When the vocabulary of the room is the medium of thought rather than a set of claims inside it, there is nothing to check a claim *against*.[^lifton] A warrant never specified cannot be tested, and asking changes the register of the conversation.

**The room cannot be won.** The room is assessing the objector's manner, so the substance is never reached: what it is watching is a nervous system, and a nervous system is what the provocation was addressed to. Winning would require being calm immediately after being made not calm, which is the one thing the provocation exists to prevent.

**And leaving cannot be framed as the occupant's failure.** Where the tolerance is sincere, a departure confirms nothing: the person who leaves is simply a person who left, and the seat survives because it was never theirs to defend. Where the tolerance is *not* sincere, the reader is in [ahead of the story](/ahead-of-the-story/)'s territory — the account is pre-discredited, the pivot has already worked the room, and every step through the open door is read. Either way the seat is not put in question.

Five exits, not one of them a way of raising the question. That is what a double bind is, and it is why the useful thing here is not a better argument but a **channel** outside the exchange.

## 9. The lineage, in this blog's own terms

**The seat, occupied without the claim.** [The owned cosmos](/the-owned-cosmos/) described a frame that names an endpoint and stations a mediator at it: the seat "can be entered by anyone, and the seat makes whatever they do to occupy it look like cosmology." That seat is held by a **claim** — a correspondence, a rite, a lineage. This post's is held by nothing at all: the same place with the claim removed, and the removal makes it harder to see, not easier, because a seat that produces no credentials produces nothing to check.

**Ahead of the story, run live.** That post documented the reversal at the scale of a network: the report is not suppressed, it is *read* through a frame that was already there, and the reading does the discrediting. This is that reversal in one room, with one substitution. There the discrediting is done by a circulating story; here it is done by the challenger's own nervous system, in public, in real time.

**The pre-installed appraisal, at one-to-one scale.** [The coercion post](/manufacturing-the-collapse/) established that the reading a group installs is not invented: it is already in the wider culture, framed as kindness, so a group "does not have to invent the story in which the collapse means progress." The culture already supplies the appraisal that an angry person is not to be trusted on the subject of their own anger — present before anyone arrives, so the move this post describes is not its author but its **beneficiary**.

**The costume's boundary, held.** [The costume](/the-costume/) exists to prevent exactly the misreading this material invites: "Levers without the mechanism are not predation", and stripped of the mechanism the levers "are merely **power**". Every element above — a seat held by silence, a manner policed, a small provocation — is a lever that runs in ordinary life with nothing behind it. What makes it this blog's subject is the joint: an **assumed** seat that makes the objection illegitimate, *and* a provocation sized below its reaction that makes the objection unwinnable. Remove either half and what remains is ordinary power, which is not this. The dissolving rule holds from the two posts that carried it: [the follower](/the-follower/) found the follower to be "a position that a condition has activated", and the label post's discipline is that describing a structure is not a finding about anyone's mind.[^label]

## 10. The boundary, and the one test this post offers

This is the closest of the three posts in this cluster to a manual, so the boundary is stated and then held.

**It describes the move, not a person.** There is no incident here, no dating, and no illustration outside the cited literature: an anecdote invites the reader to match it against someone, which is the inference this post refuses.

**There is no gauge, no list, and no way to identify who is doing this.** No technique for reading a particular person, no list of which rooms have assumed seats — every room does, which is the point — and nothing that would help anyone locate an occupant. Clause one is *nothing was said*: a description of nothing, made specific, becomes a description of a person's character.

**The behaviour is contested where it is contested.** "Reactive abuse" is not a settled construct, and its scholarly neighbours are criticised on record.[^gilchrist] Nothing here validates any particular use of the term.

**Describing a structure is not a licence to sort people into a verdict.** [The label post](/the-label/)'s rule holds: the distance between a description and a warrant is maintained by discipline, not by having the right words.[^label] A page that turned this into *how to spot who is doing this* would have reproduced the inference the coercion post documents — from *this is what it looks like* to *therefore this person cannot choose*.

Which leaves one instrument, and it has to be impersonal because nothing else can be offered honestly:

> **In this disagreement, who has to keep their temper, and who does not?**

That is checkable without judging anyone's character, because it is a claim about the distribution of a burden. If both parties are equally obliged not to raise their voice, there is no asymmetry and this post has nothing to say. If one party's heat is treated as information while the other's is treated as noise — the *manner* attended to on one side, the *substance* on the other — the room has already installed the first clause.

## 11. The countermeasure: the record held outside the room

The countermeasure is not what it first appears, because the obvious answer fails structurally.

**It is not confrontation.** Confrontation is the input the mechanism wants, on both clauses at once: it supplies the objector's remedial burden and the reaction the room has been invited to watch.

**And it is not only the in-room counter, which is real but loses.** Naming the size flatly, without heat — *you have just done something small, and I am about to feel something large about it, and both of those are true* — is accurate, metacommunicative in Bateson's sense, and the correct thing to say. It also requires being **not hot immediately after being made hot**, which is exactly the capacity the provocation was designed to remove. It therefore tends to win the moment and lose the record: an argument not against saying it, but against depending on it.

What the structure points to instead is a channel: the prohibition Bateson describes is on metacommunication, and the pathology is the loss of the **self-correcting** loop.[^bateson] The repair has to restore that loop somewhere else.

**The record, not the argument.** What the mechanism has no answer to is an *account* — dated, specific, held as a thing rather than spoken as a move. It runs on the live read: a state exists now, and a record exists at a different time, which is the dimension in which the technique has no purchase. The provocation's size can be disputed; the *date* cannot, and a sequence of dated entries describes a sequence rather than claiming anything about anyone's character — which an assumed seat cannot absorb, being maintained by the absence of a legible account.[^record]

**Outside the room, and not present in it.** The reading that matters is the room's, so the holder's defining property is not sympathy but that they are **not in the exchange** — not a party to the heat, not required to adjudicate it, and not someone whose support can be won by taking a side. The check in [the operative master](/the-operative-master/) is "a reading held by someone who gains nothing from it, and who is not *him*"; [ahead of the story](/ahead-of-the-story/)'s is "someone who knew you before, outside the network, who holds your account as credible." In a room there may be no such person, so this post's version is narrower: a **date, a sequence and a page**. Two limits go in the body. A record is not an instrument — it is only as good as its dates — and the countermeasure does not require anyone to be right: it keeps the account of **cause** alive where the live read cannot reach.

## 12. Why the fragments are the finding

Assemble the names, and the shape of the assembly is the result.

| the field | the name it has |
|---|---|
| sociology | the **working consensus**, "a real agreement as to whose claims ... will be temporarily honored"[^goffman1959] |
| sociology | the **remedial interchange**, and the apology that splits the self[^goffman1971] |
| organisational communication | **strategic ambiguity**, whose functions include "to foster deniability"[^eisenberg] |
| personality psychology | **Machiavellianism**, "guile, deceit, and opportunism in interpersonal relations"[^mach] |
| the psychiatry of totalism | **loading the language**; **doctrine over person**[^lifton] |
| psychoanalytic social theory | **character**, "the specific structure in which human energy is organized"[^fromm] |
| communication theory | the **double bind**, and the prohibition on metacommunicating[^bateson] |
| management | **abusive supervision**; **petty tyranny**[^tepper] [^ashforth] |
| developmental psychology | **indirect** and **relational** aggression[^brendgen] [^crick] |
| sociology | **gaslighting**, "primarily a sociological rather than a psychological phenomenon"[^sweet] |
| philosophy; education research | **testimonial injustice**; **tone policing**[^fricker] [^bailey] [^biddle] |
| the abuse literature | **reactive abuse** (contested); **violent resistance**[^gilchrist] [^johnson] |

The finding is not that the move is unknown. It is that **no one field holds the whole of it**: sociology has the seat and not the ratio; communication theory the levels and not the room; management the items and not the levels; the abuse literature the ratio and not the seat. Which answers the question a person inside this asks, and it is not *what is happening* but **where do I point** — there is no place, because there is no single name, and each available name is either too small (an apology, a rumour, a tone) or too large (a cult, a diagnosis, a label). The fragmentation is not an accident of scholarship: a move whose first clause is *nothing was said* and whose second is *a relation between two sizes* has no object to hold.

Which returns to the title, because both senses are the move. A seat is **assumed** when someone sits in it, and assumed in the other sense when everyone treats it as settled without having established anything. The person in it did not take it, exactly. They were allowed to have it, by the ordinary courtesy of people who would rather not make a scene — and the room's preference not to make a scene is what the second clause is calibrated to. The move is not an office anyone filled. It is a place nobody emptied.
