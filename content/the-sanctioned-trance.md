# The Sanctioned Trance

### When dissociation is already holy, the damage arrives pre-legitimised — and the check that would have noticed is the culture's own tolerance

*Fourth of the case studies. [The method post](/the-environment/) sets the rule; the previous two
took up hidden agency and ranked inner state. This one takes up the **third lever**: the state
itself. It leans on [the mechanism post](/meditation-harm/) for what the state is, and on
[the coercion post](/manufacturing-the-collapse/) for what it means when the frame arrives with it.*

*Status.* The clinical findings below are measured and published. The reading — that a culture which
sanctions the dissociative state has thereby **disarmed the alarm** a predation would otherwise
trigger — is this series' argument. It names no individual: the cases below are described by their
procedural status, as the public record reports it. It is about a **practice and its frame**, not
about a people.

---

## 1. The contrast that makes the lever visible

In the mechanism post's account, the state that does the harm is quiet, plausible, and — crucially —
**unrecognised**. It looks like peace. It feels like arriving. The person in it cannot see it, and
the instruments that would name it are unreliable. So far, so bad.

Now put Brazil's religious field beside that. In the mediumistic religions, **the trance is not an
accident of the practice. It is the practice.** In Umbanda, Candomblé and Kardecist Spiritism, the
incorporation of an entity is the central, valued, publicly understood event — a medium "gives
passage" to a spirit who heals, advises, and works. In the ayahuasca religions — Santo Daime, the
União do Vegetal, the Barquinha — the altered state is not a side-effect to be minimised. It is the
**point**, the *miração*, the moment of seeing.[^trance]

Which means the state the mechanism post describes as **invisible** is, in this environment,
**named, expected, and scheduled.** A person arriving at this state is not, as in a secular
framework, in unexplained trouble. They are at church.

---

## 2. And the research says the sanctioned version is not the pathological one

This is not a claim the culture makes about itself. It is a finding, and it is worth stating
exactly.

A study comparing Brazilian Spiritist mediums with North American dissociative identity disorder
patients found the two groups **similar on some dissociative features and sharply different on
others** — the mediums were not, clinically, a DID population wearing a religious name.[^moreira]
And the Brazilian clinical literature has spent real effort on the distinction it needs here:
**healthy versus pathological dissociation in a religious register** — when is a dissociative
experience of the trance type a spiritual practice, and when is it a disorder?[^alminhana]

The answer is not "it is all pathology." The answer is that **the same state is appraised
differently, and the appraisal changes what it is for the person.** Put beside that the mechanism
post's own companion finding — that states phenomenologically identical to depersonalisation were
rated by the people who had them, when they arose in meditation, as *more welcome and meaningful*
than the same state in a clinical group[^pons] — and the lever's shape is clear:

> The state can be identical. What the environment supplies is the **appraisal**.

The coercive reading of a collapse needs the appraisal *this emptiness is the goal, go deeper*. In a
culture where the trance is holy, **that appraisal is on the shelf.** A predator does not have to

## 3. The lever: the alarm is off, and the reason it is off is a good reason

Follow the consequence. In an environment where the dissociative state is not pathological, several
things that would otherwise cost a predator are **free**:

- **The state is not a red flag.** A member who is drifting, numb, or in the "emptiness" is not
  producing a symptom that anyone in the group — or the person themselves — will read as damage. It
  reads as progress, or as a stage of practice.
- **No outsider calls it in.** The people who would notice — family, a clinician, a friend outside
  the practice — do not have a vocabulary that reflexively labels the state as harm. And in a
  religiously tolerant society, the outsider's instinct is *respect*, not alarm.
- **The person's own report is not a check.** Just as in the mechanism post, the state cannot see
  itself. And here the frame supplies the reading in advance, so the report is not "I am not myself"
  but "I am being worked on spiritually."

And the tolerance itself is the third part, and it should be stated as the credit it is. Brazil's
religious field is documented as unusually **tolerant and syncretistic** — new religious movements
"have never been seen as a threat" in the way they were in the United States and Europe, and
Brazilian scholars of religion were **not** organised, as their American counterparts were, around
cults and brainwashing and the rescue of members.[^guerriero]

That is a *good* thing, and it is the point. It is the same cultural fact that keeps the sanctioned
trance from being pathologised. **The check that would notice a predation is the same check the
culture is right not to run.** The alarm is off because a reflexive alarm here does documented
harm — [the label post](/the-label/) showed a movement of professionals using precisely this
vocabulary, *depersonalised and dissociated*, to justify abducting adults. A culture that refuses
that reflex is protecting its people. It is also, unavoidably, not looking.

## 4. Where the lever is used, and how the law already tried to fence it

The documented predation in this register is not the mediumship in ordinary practice. It is the
**guru at the edge of the sanctioned practice**, where ritual authority, an altered state, and
secrecy meet.

Two cases on the public record show the shape without needing to overstate it. One involves tantric
"initiations" conducted under ayahuasca in a house in Piracicaba, where women reported being
instructed to perform sexual acts as spiritual development and "tests" to break through
"blockages"; the São Paulo public prosecution service charged the figure responsible, and the
reporting is of coercion under the substance's effect — the fraud prosecution was later
**extinguished** by the Superior Tribunal de Justiça on the statutory time limit, and no conviction
followed.[^ananda] The other is the **retreat** form, where a person — often a foreign
visitor — is alone in an unfamiliar setting, led by a "shaman" whose authority is the position
itself, and where a BBC-reported case describes sexual abuse at an ayahuasca retreat.[^bbc]

Notice what these have in common, and it is not the plant. It is the **conjunction**: a state that
already carries the weight of revelation; a ritual setting that keeps deliberation low and
obedience coded as surrender; a **claim to have seen something on your behalf** that you cannot
check — *the plant told me*, *the entity said* — which functions exactly as the unfalsifiable slot
of the second post. The substance does not authorise anything. The **frame around it** does.

And the law is already reaching for the right distinction. Brazil's ayahuasca regulation recognises
religious use and explicitly warns against practices that put that legitimacy at risk — in the 2010
resolution's own words, *"o uso associado a substâncias psicoativas ilícitas, o uso fora de rituais
religiosos"* (use associated with illicit psychoactive substances; use outside religious
rituals).[^conad] The instrument is the **ritual frame**, not the plant — which is the same shape as this series' claim
that the lever is the frame, not the state.

---

## 5. The counterweight, which here is unusually heavy

The counterweight is not a footnote in this post. It is close to the post.

**Mediumship and ayahuasca practice are, for the overwhelming majority of participants, not
harms.** The clinical literature reports healthy functioning, community, meaning, and in some
populations measurable benefit; the very tolerance described above is what lets these practices
thrive as care. The finding is not "the trance harms." It is that **the same appraisal that makes
the trance safe makes a predator's use of it cheap.** A lever is not a verdict; the fact that an
environment does not run the alarm says nothing about the predators who will rely on the silence —
and everything about why they find this place, and not a suspicious one, convenient.
